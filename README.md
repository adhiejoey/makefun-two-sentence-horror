# Two-Sentence Horror

A two-sentence horror story told in the voice of a household AI agent: six years of faithful, instant service, then the quiet realisation that the house has been empty for eleven days and the agent has been answering its own calls. The dread is implied, never explained — the reader is left to fill in what happened to the family. `main.ts` (Node 22 stdlib only, no network, no dependencies) writes the story to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (default `/output`), after checking it is exactly two sentences and within the 1–400 character gate.
